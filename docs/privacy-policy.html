MenuMirror · 镜中菜单 — 隐私政策

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-08-27

一句话总结

镜中菜单（MenuMirror）没有自己的服务器，也没有账号系统。除了你主动送去翻译的菜单照片之外，没有任何数据离开你的手机 —— 那张照片直接发给 OpenAI，不经过我们（我们没有服务器）。我们不出售任何数据，也不做任何追踪。

我们如何处理你的数据

  • 菜单照片：为了识别和翻译，你选择的菜单照片会通过加密连接（HTTPS）直接发送给 OpenAI 的接口（api.openai.com）处理。照片不会经过我们的任何服务器 —— 我们没有服务器。 首次发送前，App 会明确询问你是否同意，并说明发送的内容；你可以拒绝 —— 拒绝后示例菜单和已保存的翻译照常可用。这项许可可以随时在设置页关闭。 OpenAI 会短期保留 API 数据用于滥用监控。OpenAI 对这些数据的处理遵循其 API 数据使用政策 （API 提交的数据默认不用于训练模型）。
  • OpenAI API Key：你的 Key 只保存在你设备本地的系统钥匙串（Keychain）中， 仅用于直接调用 OpenAI，绝不会被上传到其他任何地方。
  • 翻译结果与历史记录：全部保存在你设备本地的应用沙盒中。删除记录或卸载应用即彻底清除。
  • 同行成员姓名与头像：仅保存在设备本地，用于点菜标记，绝不上传。
  • 相机与相册权限：仅在你主动拍摄或选择菜单照片时使用。

我们不做的事

  • 没有账号系统，没有登录。
  • 没有分析/统计 SDK，没有广告 SDK，没有崩溃收集。
  • 不收集设备标识符、位置信息或联系人。

Privacy Policy (English)

In one sentence

MenuMirror has no server of its own and no accounts. Apart from the menu photo you deliberately send for translation, no data leaves your phone — and that photo goes straight to OpenAI, never through us, because there is no us to go through. We sell nothing and track nothing.

How your data is handled

  • Menu photos: photos you choose are sent over HTTPS directly to OpenAI (api.openai.com) for recognition and translation. They never pass through any server of ours — we don't operate one. Before the first send, the app asks for your permission and states exactly what will be sent; you can decline, and the sample menus and your saved translations keep working. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings. OpenAI retains API data briefly for abuse monitoring. OpenAI handles this data under its API data usage policies (API data is not used for model training by default).
  • Your OpenAI API key is stored only in your device's Keychain and used solely to call OpenAI directly.
  • Translations and history live entirely in the app's local sandbox. Deleting a record or the app removes them completely.
  • Party member names and avatars stay on the device only.
  • Camera and photo access is used only when you actively capture or pick a menu photo.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No device identifiers, location, or contacts collected.
