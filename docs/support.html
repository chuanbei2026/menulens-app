这个 App 做什么

拍一张外语菜单的照片，它把整份菜单按原来的版式翻译成你的语言 —— 译文写在菜单原来的位置上，字号、行距、排布都保留。菜名保留原文对照，点菜时可以直接 把手机递给服务员。支持中文（简体）、English、日本語、한국어、Français、Español、हिन्दी 七种语言。

账号与点数

不需要登录：第一次打开时 App 自动给这台设备建一个本机账号，新账号送 300 点，用完可以在 App 里的商店（设置页 →「账号」→「买点数」）通过 Apple 购买： 100 点 $0.99、500 点 $4.99、2000 点 $19.99（美区价格）。点数永不过期。不需要你自己的 OpenAI API Key。 想在其他设备上用同一份余额，可以在 设置 →「账号」里用 Apple 登录（可选，不要姓名、不要邮箱）， 本机的余额会并入你的 Apple 账号。有 App Store 优惠码的话，在商店页点「兑换 App Store 优惠码」。

一份菜单要花多少点

一页菜单约 6–12 点，取决于菜单的密度和你选的模型。为没有照片的菜生成配图另计： 拼图模式一张四宫格（四道菜）约 2–3 点。设置页的「账号」里能看到余额。

不登录能用吗

可以，所有功能都不需要登录：识别菜单、商店、购买和会员都直接用本机账号。 App 还内置两家真实餐厅的完整示例，点主界面左上角的时钟图标即可查看。

照片会被发到哪里

发到我们的服务器，再由服务器转交 OpenAI 翻译，且只在你点「识别并翻译」时。 服务器不保存照片和文字。首次发送前 App 会明确询问你是否同意，并列出发送的内容； 你可以拒绝，也可以随时在设置页关闭。详见 隐私政策。

怎么删除账号

设置页 →「账号」→「删除账号」（本机账号和 Apple 账号都可以删）。账号和余额会从服务器上删除；手机上的翻译记录不受影响。

常见问题

  • 识别失败 / 报错：先看设置页的余额是否够用；提示 Apple 登录过期就在设置页重新登录。报错原文会显示在 App 里，把它连同截图发给我们。
  • 翻译得不准：设置页可以换模型；gpt-5 最准但最慢。菜单照片拍正、拍清楚也能明显提升效果。
  • 想换界面语言：设置页里的「语言」开关同时切换界面语言和翻译目标语言。
  • 历史记录存在哪：全部在你手机本地。删除记录或卸载 App 即彻底清除。

What the app does

Photograph a foreign menu and get it back translated in place — the translation is written where the original text sat, with the layout, type size and line spacing preserved. Dish names stay bilingual, so you can hand the phone to your server. Seven languages: 中文（简体）, English, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Español, हिन्दी.

Account and credits

No sign-in needed: on first launch the app opens an account for this device. New accounts get 300 free credits; after that, buy more through Apple in the in-app shop (Settings → Account → Buy credits): 100 credits for $0.99, 500 for $4.99, 2000 for $19.99 (US prices). Credits never expire. You do not need your own OpenAI API key. To use the same balance on other devices, sign in with Apple in Settings → Account (optional; no name, no email) and the device's balance moves into your Apple account. Have an App Store offer code? Tap "Redeem App Store Offer Code" in the shop.

What does a menu cost?

About 6–12 credits a page, depending on how dense the menu is and which model you pick. Generated dish photos are extra: in collage mode one 2×2 picture (four dishes) is about 2–3 credits. Your balance is under Settings → Account.

Can I use it without signing in?

Yes — nothing needs signing in: scanning, the shop, purchases and the membership all use the device account. Two complete real restaurant menus are bundled too — tap the clock icon at the top left of the home screen.

Where do my photos go?

To our server, which passes them to OpenAI for the translation — and only when you tap Scan. Our server does not store photos or text. Before the first send the app asks for your permission and states exactly what will be sent; you can decline, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account (device or Apple account). The account and its balance are removed from our server; the translations on your phone stay.

Troubleshooting

  • Scan fails or errors: check your balance in Settings; if the app says your Apple sign-in expired, sign in again in Settings. The error text is shown in the app — send it to us with a screenshot.
  • Translation is off: try another model in Settings; gpt-5 is the most accurate and the slowest. A straight, sharp photo also helps a lot.
  • Changing the interface language: the "Language" switch in Settings sets both the interface and the translation target.
  • Where is my history stored?: entirely on your phone. Deleting a record or the app removes it completely.
