这个 App 做什么

拍一张外语菜单的照片，它把整份菜单按原来的版式翻译成你的语言 —— 译文写在菜单原来的位置上，字号、行距、排布都保留。菜名保留原文对照，点菜时可以直接 把手机递给服务员。支持中文（简体）、English、日本語、한국어、Français、Español、हिन्दी 七种语言。

为什么需要我自己的 OpenAI API Key

这个 App 没有服务器。照片直接从你的手机发给 OpenAI 处理，不经过我们 （我们没有可以经过的东西）。这样做的代价是需要你自己的 API Key，好处是我们看不到、 也存不下你的任何数据。

获取方式：在 platform.openai.com/api-keys 注册、充值，创建一个 Key，粘贴进 App 的设置页。Key 只保存在你手机的系统钥匙串里。

一份菜单要花多少钱

约 $0.05–0.25，取决于菜单的密度和你选的模型。为没有照片的菜生成配图 另计，约 $0.003 一道。设置页里写了各模型实测的耗时与成本。

不填 Key 能用吗

可以。App 内置两家真实餐厅的完整示例，点主界面左上角的时钟图标即可查看， 所有功能（缩放画布、菜品列表、多人点单、标注给服务员看、导出 PDF）都能体验。

照片会被发到哪里

只发给 OpenAI 的接口（api.openai.com），且只在你点「识别并翻译」时。 首次发送前 App 会明确询问你是否同意，并列出发送的内容；你可以拒绝，也可以随时在 设置页关闭。详见 隐私政策。

常见问题

  • 识别失败 / 报错：先确认 Key 有效且账户有余额。OpenAI 的报错原文会显示在 App 里，把它连同截图发给我们。
  • 翻译得不准：设置页可以换模型；gpt-5 最准但最慢。菜单照片拍正、拍清楚也能明显提升效果。
  • 想换界面语言：设置页里的「语言」开关同时切换界面语言和翻译目标语言。
  • 历史记录存在哪：全部在你手机本地。删除记录或卸载 App 即彻底清除。

What the app does

Photograph a foreign menu and get it back translated in place — the translation is written where the original text sat, with the layout, type size and line spacing preserved. Dish names stay bilingual, so you can hand the phone to your server. Seven languages: 中文（简体）, English, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Español, हिन्दी.

Why do I need my own OpenAI API key?

The app has no server. Photos go straight from your phone to OpenAI; nothing passes through us, because there is no us to pass through. The cost is that you bring your own key; the benefit is that we can neither see nor store any of your data.

Get one at platform.openai.com/api-keys: sign up, add credit, create a key, paste it into the app's Settings. The key is stored only in your phone's Keychain.

What does a menu cost?

About $0.05–0.25, depending on how dense the menu is and which model you pick. Generated dish photos are billed separately, about $0.003 each. Settings lists measured timings and costs per model.

Can I use it without a key?

Yes. Two complete real restaurant menus are bundled — tap the clock icon at the top left of the home screen. Every feature works on them: the zoomable in-place canvas, the dish list, group ordering, annotating the menu for your server, and PDF export.

Where do my photos go?

Only to OpenAI's API (api.openai.com), and only when you tap Scan. Before the first send the app asks for your permission and states exactly what will be sent; you can decline, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

  • Scan fails or errors: check the key is valid and the account has credit. OpenAI's own error text is shown in the app — send it to us with a screenshot.
  • Translation is off: try another model in Settings; gpt-5 is the most accurate and the slowest. A straight, sharp photo also helps a lot.
  • Changing the interface language: the "Language" switch in Settings sets both the interface and the translation target.
  • Where is my history stored?: entirely on your phone. Deleting a record or the app removes it completely.
